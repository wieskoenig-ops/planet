About planet

planet is a home for essays on tipping points: on systems that seem stable for a long time and then jump into a different state – and on the question of whether there is a way back.

The essays come from four fields: climate (the Atlantic circulation, ice sheets, hysteresis), politics (responsibility for processes that outlast a legislative term), technology (emergence in artificial intelligence) and markets (feedback, reflexivity, cascades).

Relation to emergenz.art

emergenz.art is the calm, explanatory sister site (in German): what is emergence, and how does it appear in the sciences and in art? It also holds the foundations these essays build on – the page on tipping points and the glossary.

planet is the part that deals with the present. The essays are dated; where the situation changes, they receive an update with a date.

Approach

The essays describe system dynamics. They do not look for culprits, they do not judge parties and they give no investment advice. Figures and studies are referenced; the sources are listed at the end of each essay.

The English versions are translations of the German originals.

Contact and legal notice

planet is run together with emergenz.art. Contact and the legal notice and privacy information (in German) apply to both sites. New essays are also available as an RSS feed.
